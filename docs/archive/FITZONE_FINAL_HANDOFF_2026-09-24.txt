CURRENT PRODUCT SCOPE
---------------------
1. Adaptive fitness intelligence shared by Dashboard, AI Plan, Workout and Assistant.
2. Exact recommendation -> workout identity through recommendation_events.workout_id.
3. Authoritative workout completion remains in existing workout completion state.
4. Goal-aware recommendations and personalized workout generation path.
5. Real daily/weekly/monthly/yearly activity history with timezone-aware dates.
6. FitZone activity matrix / heatmap based only on saved workout activity.
7. Dynamic date/day greeting and real weekly activity summaries.
8. Natural-language nutrition logging with deterministic nutrient calculations.
9. Nutrition food matching, household-portion normalization, review-before-save and confidence states.
10. Nutrition timeline, history, personalized planning target state and AI nutrition actions.
11. Nutrition adherence ML with honest cold-start behavior until trained on sufficient real history.
12. Unified workout + nutrition state available to the intelligence layer.
13. Protected admin/owner console with member directory, membership management, analytics, audit log and CSV export.
14. Admin-only nutrition ML training from real member history.
15. Hardened authentication/session validation, protected routes, rate limits and security headers.
16. Logout clears session only; saved database history is preserved.

RECOMMENDED LOCAL ACCEPTANCE FLOW
---------------------------------
1. Log in with a real Supabase account.
2. Open Goals and save a goal.
3. Confirm Dashboard / AI Plan use the saved goal.
4. Generate a new workout and confirm its workout identity is linked to the recommendation event.
5. Complete the workout through the normal Workout page.
6. Confirm Dashboard / Progress update from the saved completion timestamp.
7. Open Nutrition and enter a natural-language meal such as "2 eggs, 2 chapatis and 150g chicken curry".
8. Review the parsed items, quantities, totals and confidence before saving.
9. Confirm the new meal appears in Today's nutrition and in the daily activity state.
10. Switch the Nutrition/Dashboard history range (7D/4W/3M/1Y) and confirm the graph changes to the selected server-derived history.
11. Log out and log back in; confirm the saved history remains.
12. For the owner account, open Admin Console and confirm member data, membership state, audit history and nutrition ML status.
13. Apply a membership change and confirm it appears in the audit trail.
14. Train nutrition ML only when enough real member history exists; a failure caused by insufficient class diversity/history is expected and must not be bypassed with synthetic training data.

NO-FAKE-DATA RULE
-----------------
FitZone does not manufacture calories, activity, progress, streaks, member counts, AI outcomes or trained-model claims.
When evidence is insufficient, the UI should show an honest unavailable / not-enough-data state.

NUTRITION DATA NOTE
-------------------
backend/data/foods.json is a curated reference catalog, not a complete import of USDA or ICMR-NIN datasets. Values are rounded tracking/planning references and household portions are estimates. Verify licensing before any commercial dataset expansion.
